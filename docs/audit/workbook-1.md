# Audit: workbook, month 1

Source: «Таджвид для начинающих». Рабочая тетрадь, 1 месяц. Международный университет Корана. File `1 РТ.pdf`, 32 pages, images only.
Audited: 2026-10-24, page by page. Status: **structure audited; methodological review and rights pending.**

This is a map of the structure written in our own words. It does not reproduce the workbook's wording, illustrations or exercise lists.

## Findings

- The workbook covers 10 lessons, numbered **6, 7, 8, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 18, 19**.
- **Lessons 1–5, 9, 10, 15 and 17 are not in the file.** They may be introductory, oral or review sessions held without the workbook. We need confirmation from the owner or teacher; the specification forbids inventing them.
- Every letter page uses the same pattern: letter name, list of characteristics, a 6-column table of positions (alone, initial, middle after a joining letter, end after a joining letter, middle and end after a non-joining letter), then copying practice with the three short vowels and sukun.
- Recurring exercise types: copying (handwriting grid); matching a letter to its name; matching a letter to its characteristics; finding a letter or vowel in short surahs (An-Nas, Al-Falaq, Al-Masad, An-Nasr, Al-Fatiha, Al-Kawthar, Quraysh, Az-Zalzala, Al-Humaza, Al-Qadr, Al-Kafirun, Al-Layl); reading syllables and words aloud; observing articulation in a mirror.
- Characteristics vocabulary used throughout: «гордая / дружелюбная» (non-joining / joining), звонкая / глухая, мягкая / твёрдая, короткая / средняя / долгая на сукуне, калькаля, межзубная, свистящая, вибрирующая.
